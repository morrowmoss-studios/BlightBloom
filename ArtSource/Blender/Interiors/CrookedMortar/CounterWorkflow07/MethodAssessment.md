# Counter workflow experiment 07 — assessment

Baseline: existing Git checkpoint 2dd2625; Production02 is preserved. Superseded Reconstruction06 installation is recovered outside Assets and removed from the working scene. Its incomplete sources remain; they are not approved output.

## Root limitations found

- AF01→HR02→LI03→C05 builders reuse box/lathe helpers and join them into material-batched meshes. Beveling a repeated construction recipe does not supply an authored silhouette.
- Broad planar UV projection samples small shared atlas regions. A narrow walnut patch is stretched over unrelated forms; the texture cannot describe a specific worn edge, recessed field or carved leaf.
- Tiny stems, thin relief leaves and printed applique do not become convincing joinery merely by accumulating them. Existing layers can duplicate furniture bodies and obscure the intended hierarchy.
- Most shape decisions precede no asset-specific drawing, sculpt or paint study. Export success and collider tests cannot measure beauty.

## Methods considered

- More primitive detailing: rejected as the main approach; repeats the failure.
- Subdivision everywhere: rejected; smooths weak design and inflates geometry.
- Reference-guided curves/profile surfaces plus genuinely sculpted panel fields: chosen for silhouette and construction; each support/profile needs a designed cross-section and bearing relationship.
- Purpose-made relief height artwork, high-resolution displacement and reduced gameplay mesh, with bespoke aligned painted colour: chosen for rich continuous wood carving without loose applique geometry. Height must be a separate intentional depth asset, not albedo brightness treated as depth.
- Asset-specific UVs and a dedicated painted base-colour map: chosen; do not use lighting or additional props to manufacture improvement.
- Full manual sculpt/retopology/texture paint by an environment artist: highest control and appropriate next step if this assisted experiment still misses the target; currently available Blender supports it, but automation does not replace trained artistic judgement.
- Image-to-3D services: no configured capability found in the current tool set. Do not buy/install or upload to an external service without approval. A generated image is not a finished game mesh.

Chosen experiment: a single bespoke counter, fitted to the existing 2.28m × 0.78m envelope and 1.0525m work-surface datum. Compare in a separate copy of Production02 under identical camera, light and dressing conditions. Keep the original counter/scene/assets intact. No additional loose props.
