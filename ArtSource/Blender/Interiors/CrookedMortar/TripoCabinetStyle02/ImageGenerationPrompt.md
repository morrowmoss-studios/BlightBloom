## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual diffuse color texture for Blender wooden furniture, not a furniture illustration. Create one square seamless tile of warm medium-dark walnut wood, grain running vertically. Painterly 3D fantasy illustration material: tactile, hand-painted broad flowing grain ribbons with subtle irregular fine brush strokes, warm umber and muted honey brown variation. Close, even, low-contrast color groups, softly worn craftsmanship. Rich and grounded rather than cartoon or photorealistic. This will be layered onto an existing detailed medieval botanical medicine cabinet. Orthographic flat texture only, evenly lit albedo, no perspective, no cast shadows, no specular highlights, no bevels, no planks or seams, no objects, leaves, ornament, labels or borders. Grain should be graceful and hand-authored rather than dense noisy scratches. Retain enough midtone contrast for directional wood grain to read on shelves at gameplay distance. Do not add damage, black cracks, white scratches or photographic noise.

## Application

Texture is layered into the original registered UV atlas through the editable Blender material graph and baked, not used as an image projection or substituted for geometry. Seamlessness was requested but is not guaranteed mathematically; limited blend contribution and inherited construction variation reduce obvious repetition. The source itself was inspected before use.
