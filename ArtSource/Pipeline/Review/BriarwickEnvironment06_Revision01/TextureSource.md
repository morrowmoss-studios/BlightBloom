# Short turf source

Method: built-in ImageGen, one new image; inspected before use. No external paid service or package installation. Imported source is preserved separately from compressed Unity texture.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tiling opaque diffuse/albedo ground texture for Unity Terrain, square1024x1024. Primary request: continuous closely growing SHORT meadow grass with subtly visible warm dry soil and humus between tiny grass strokes, for a softly painted medieval countryside. Orthographic straight-down surface scan, fills entire square edge-to-edge. Rich hand-painted fantasy illustration, tactile but restrained, muted olive/sage/soft straw greens and warm grey-brown earth, middle values, gentle organic color transitions. Small overlapping tapered brush strokes suggest short dense turf at ground level rather than separate tufts. Broad low-contrast variations, no prominent central features or recognizable repeating clusters. Soft even neutral diffuse lighting: no cast shadows, no sun direction, no specular, no ambient occlusion baked strongly. Seamless opposite edges intended to tile. No flowers, trees, rocks, stones, paths, props, text, border, vignette, perspective, tall grass, large leaves, neon green, photographic noise, harsh dark crevices or cartoon shapes. Ground must read as a continuous living grassy surface beneath sparse mesh plants, never a collection of isolated grass islands.

Result:1254² source, imported at1024². Aiming for seamless output is not proof of invisible tiling. Reviewed close/ground-level views reveal a repeating stroke pattern; listed as artistic limitation.
