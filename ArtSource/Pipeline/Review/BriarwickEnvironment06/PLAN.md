Scene-global lighting, camera, animation, paths and architecture unchanged. Distant appearance through broad ground pattern, not unbounded detail-distance increase.

Available nature/terrain prefabs contain grass/ferns/shrubs/flowering plants; no verified loose rock/log/root or dedicated reed meshes. Branch-named textures are tree canopy sources, not standalone debris. Do not substitute architectural rock-floor meshes. Document these limits.
